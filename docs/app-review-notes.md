field on App Store Connect when submitting.

Hi Apple review team,

SE7A is an AI food and fitness coach for the Gulf region. Below is
what you need to test the app in ~5 minutes.

## Sign in

SE7A uses passwordless magic-link authentication. There is no traditional
password. Two options:

1. **Use the demo account we've pre-provisioned:**
   - Email: `apple-review@se7a.app`
   - Contact `hello@se7a.app` for a one-time login link that stays valid
     throughout the review period.

2. **Use any email you like** — enter it on the login screen, tap the
   link in your inbox. First-time email lands you in the onboarding
   wizard.

## Core flows to try

1. **Onboarding** — 11 short questions. Auto-fills for returning users.
2. **Log tab → Scan a plate** — take any photo of food (or use the
   photo library). Grants camera + photo library permission on first
   use. AI returns calorie and macro RANGES (not point values) —
   this is intentional; see "Honest ranges" below.
3. **Progress tab → Log a weigh-in** — enter weight in kg. Chart
   updates.
4. **Coach tab** — chat interface. Ask "what should I eat for lunch?"
   or "is shawarma OK tonight?" Response is generated by Anthropic's
   Claude API server-side.
5. **Home tab → gear icon → Settings** — includes Terms, Privacy,
   Sign out, and **Delete my account** (per your requirement §5.1.1).

## HealthKit

SE7A auto-imports weight and body fat from Apple Health on first
launch of the Home tab. Permission is requested via the standard
iOS sheet. Purpose is stated in the Info.plist usage descriptions:

- `NSHealthShareUsageDescription`: "SE7A reads your weight and workout
  history from Apple Health to keep your progress in sync."
- `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription`: "SE7A saves the weight check-ins
  you log so they show up in Apple Health."

## Push notifications

Requested on first launch of the Home tab. Used for a weekly recap
every Sunday (last week's stats). No marketing pushes.

## Widget

An optional home-screen widget shows "remaining kcal today". Reads
from a public token-authed endpoint (`/api/widget/status?token=...`).
The token is stored in shared UserDefaults (App Group
`group.app.se7a.mobile`).

## The "honest ranges" rule

Every calorie or macro estimate SE7A shows is a RANGE (e.g. "540–680
kcal"), not a single number. This is a deliberate product principle
— a photo can't see the oil, and pretending otherwise misleads users.
Please don't flag ranges as "unclear information"; they're the
correct representation of the underlying uncertainty.

## Body scan privacy

The body-scan feature analyzes a physique photo in memory server-side,
returns a body-fat range, and never stores the photo. This is a
privacy commitment stated in our `/privacy` page and enforced in
`app/api/scan/body/route.ts` (image_path is always null for kind='body'
in the scans table).

## Medical disclaimer

SE7A includes prominent disclaimers throughout that outputs are
estimates, not medical advice. The coach refuses to prescribe for
users under 16 or with BMI < 17 and redirects them to a physician.
