## Core flows to try

1. **Onboarding** — 11 short questions. If reviewer account already
   completed it, you'll land on Home directly.
2. **Log tab → Scan a plate** — take any food photo (or use the photo
   library). Grants camera + photo library permission on first use.
   AI returns calorie and macro RANGES (not point values) — intentional;
   see "Honest ranges" below.
3. **Progress tab → Log a weigh-in** — enter weight in kg. Chart updates.
4. **Coach tab (Pro)** — chat interface. Ask "what should I eat for
   lunch?" or "is shawarma OK tonight?" Response is generated by
   Anthropic Claude server-side.
5. **More → Meal plan (Pro)** — tap "Generate week" → ~15–20 sec to build.
6. **Scan tab → Menu (Pro)** — point camera at any restaurant menu.
7. **More → Body scan (Pro)** — front/side/back photos; estimates body-fat
   range. Photos are NOT stored.
8. **Settings → Delete my account** (per your requirement §5.1.1) — hard
   deletes all user data from our DB + storage buckets.

## HealthKit

SE7A auto-imports weight and body fat from Apple Health on first launch
of the Home tab. Permission requested via standard iOS sheet. Purpose
stated in Info.plist usage descriptions:

- `NSHealthShareUsageDescription`: "SE7A reads your weight and workout
  history from Apple Health to keep your progress in sync."
- `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription`: "SE7A saves the weight check-ins
  you log so they show up in Apple Health."

## Push notifications

Requested on first launch of the Home tab. Used for a daily meal
reminder (opt-in per time-of-day) and a weekly recap every Sunday
(last week's stats). No marketing pushes. User can disable each
notification type in Settings → Notifications.

## The "honest ranges" rule

Every calorie or macro estimate SE7A shows is a RANGE (e.g. "540–680
kcal"), not a single number. This is a deliberate product principle —
a photo can't see the oil, and pretending otherwise misleads users.
Please don't flag ranges as "unclear information"; they're the correct
representation of underlying uncertainty.

## Body scan privacy

The body-scan feature analyzes a physique photo in memory server-side,
returns a body-fat range, and never stores the photo. This is a privacy
commitment stated in our `/privacy` page and enforced in
`app/api/scan/body/route.ts` (image_path is always null for kind='body'
in the scans table).

## Medical disclaimer

SE7A includes prominent disclaimers throughout that outputs are estimates,
not medical advice. The coach refuses to prescribe for users under 16 or
with BMI < 17 and redirects them to a physician.

## AI providers (data flow)
